WACC Challenge

B6302 Corporate Finance · Lecture 6 · Estimate a WACC two ways: with the firm's own beta and with two peers' betas
Your team: pick a publicly traded focal firm and two peers with similar operations (not banks or insurers). From Yahoo Finance: the quote page (price, market cap, beta "5Y Monthly") and the balance sheet ("Total Debt", "Cash, Cash Equivalents & Short Term Investments", latest quarter). Type all dollar amounts in millions of USD (Yahoo shows the market cap in B or T: 1.019T = 1,019,000; 490.194B = 490,194). Then build the same numbers in your blank Excel template, check they match this page, and Download PDF to submit on Canvas.
Fixed for every team (as in the Walmart case): tax rate t = 21% · cost of debt rD = 5.76% · risk-free rate rf = 5.10% (10-year Treasury) · market risk premium = 5%. Rounding as on the slides: weights to 3 decimals; asset betas and the relevered beta to 2; the average asset beta to 3; rE and the WACC to 2 (in %).
Preview of your PDF (one page per slide). In the print window choose Save as PDF, layout Landscape, margins None; Chrome works best.
